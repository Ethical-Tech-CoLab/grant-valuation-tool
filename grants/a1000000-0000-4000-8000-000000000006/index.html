Description

An annual open call funding US 501(c) nonprofits that use technology in new or expanded ways (pilots, proofs of concept, or scaling existing programs). Three tiers: Pathfinder (up to $200K unrestricted + $100K AWS credits), Go Further Faster (up to $150K + $100K credits), Momentum to Modernize (up to $50K + $20K credits). Welcomes all mission areas and is technology-agnostic (not AI-only). 2026 Round One closed 5 June 2026 — recurring annually (portal typically opens ~March).

The AWS Imagine Grant offers attractive unrestricted funding for exactly the kind of pilots and proofs of concept ETC builds, and NYU's nonprofit status makes ETC eligible. The main caveats are moderate competitiveness in a broad open call, the tension between an AWS credit-based award and ETC's Microsoft Research infrastructure, and the fact that the current round has closed. Worth considering for the next annual cycle if ETC can frame a specific, AWS-deliverable humanitarian intervention — but not a slam-dunk priority.

Effort / feasibility63/100 · weight 15%

Strategic value55/100 · weight 15%

Red flags

  • Potential conflict/optics with ETC's existing Microsoft Research partnership — the award is AWS-funded and tied to AWS cloud credits, which pushes toward an AWS-native tech stack that may not align with ETC's current infrastructure
  • Current 2026 round already closed (5 June 2026); ETC would have to wait for the next annual cycle (~March), so this is not actionable immediately
  • Eligibility is framed around 501(c) nonprofits and their programs; a university research CoLab must apply through NYU and may be a less typical fit than a service-delivery nonprofit
  • Mission fit is broad/generic rather than aligned to ETC's core humanitarian focus areas, so the proposal must actively frame a specific intervention (evacuation, traceability, etc.) to stand out
