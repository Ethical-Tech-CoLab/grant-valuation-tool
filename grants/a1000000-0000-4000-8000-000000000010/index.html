Description

Commits up to $100M to AI-powered solutions for climate change and biodiversity, across two phases: up to 30 Phase I seed grants of $50,000 for promising ideas, then up to 15 Phase II implementation grants of up to $2M over two years. Round-one focus areas: sustainable proteins, biodiversity conservation, and power-grid optimization, plus a 'Wild Card' category. Includes mentorship, compute infrastructure, and access to AI technology providers.
