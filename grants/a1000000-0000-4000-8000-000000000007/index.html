Description

Supports nonprofits, social enterprises, and academic institutions partnering with governments to use generative and agentic AI to transform how public services function for social good. Includes funding plus the Google.org Accelerator (pro bono Google technical expertise, training, and Cloud credits). Global eligibility. The 2026 cycle deadline was 3 April 2026 — recurring.
